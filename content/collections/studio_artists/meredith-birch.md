        text: 'Meredith Birch is a Greenville, South Carolina based artist and educator whose work explores the intersection of creativity, wellness, and personal reflection. With more than a decade of experience in arts education in Massachusetts and South Carolina, her practice is grounded in the belief that creative expression can foster mindfulness, resilience, and meaningful connection. Informed by her background in arts education and community engagement, Birch has developed and facilitated creative programs that expand access to artistic practice and encourage experimentation and personal voice. Through both her studio practice and teaching, she investigates how creative processes can support personal growth while fostering connection through shared artistic experiences. She lives and works in Greenville, South Carolina and is currently expanding her studio practice while deepening her engagement with the local arts community.'
