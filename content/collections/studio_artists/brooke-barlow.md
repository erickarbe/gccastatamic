artist_quote: |-
  My work explores the intimate relationship between the human form and the natural world. I am
  drawn to the body not as an object, but as a landscape...one that holds memory, emotion and
  quiet transformation. Through clay, I investigate softness and strength, tension and release,
  presence and becoming.
  Nature is both reference and collaborator in my process. Organic textures, movement and
  imperfection inform each piece, allowing the work to feel lived-in rather than constructed. I am
  less interested in perfection and more interested in truth, the subtle gestures and forms that
  reveal something real.
  Clay became my language during a time of personal healing, and it continues to be a space
  where I return to myself. Each sculpture is an offering, an exploration of connection, both within
  and beyond the body. My hope is that the work invites a pause, a recognition and a
  remembering of our own belonging to the natural world.
