        text: 'Lisa’s personal philosophy: there’s beauty in the overlooked. Subjects don’t need to be grand or theatrical. Subjects can be jars, marbles, reflections, shadows. Something compelling can be found in ordinary things. Being an artist gives her an excuse to find the most interesting part of everything. Anyone who knows her has heard her say, "I would continue to paint even if I never sold another painting. Yet I feel both humbled and full of gratitude when someone chooses to hang one of my paintings in their home."'
