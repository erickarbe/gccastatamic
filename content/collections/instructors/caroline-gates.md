        text: 'A furtive glance, a fleeting sigh, a soft touch–all point to something which shimmers in the air between us, something that’s been felt before and that will be felt again. My paintings dwell in these charged moments, exploring the nuance of human connection. They revolve around human relationships from a woman’s point of view, revealing the layered complexities of longing, vulnerability, power, disdain. These works speak to the familiar ache for intimacy and the uncertainty of how to navigate it. They ask what it takes to be seen, to be heard, to be known.'

        type: text
        text: 'At the heart of these paintings is an exploration of psychological space, both the environments where the figures find themselves and the charged atmosphere between one body and the next. A hand on a shoulder, the refusal of a gaze, the press of an embrace: these simple gestures shape the invisible distances between people as much as they suggest physical closeness. They create the tension which permeates human relationships and seem to physically alter the way we experience the world.'

        type: text
        text: 'The largely minimalist settings in the paintings are stripped to the essentials, painted in support of the emotional narrative. At times, the environment disappears entirely, while in other works, the landscape comes alive in soft, twisting brushstrokes. By stripping away extraneous detail, I invite viewers into a moment with just enough information to unravel the narrative before them, still holding space for ambiguity.'
  -
    type: paragraph
    content:

        type: text
        text: 'My painting style reflects the balance between clarity and haze. Working in oil, I build surfaces with layers of glazes and impasto, creating a luminosity that allows figures to emerge or dissolve in turn. Brushwork shifts from delicate precision to long, loose strokes, suggesting how our vision oscillates between sharp recognition and blurred uncertainty. Color is restrained but resonant, often used symbolically to draw the eye and support the narrative.'

    type: paragraph
    content:

        type: text
        text: 'In each of these paintings, the figures exist suspended in the breath before the next gesture is made. They hover on the cusp of a moment alive with the possibility of closeness or retreat. In this charged stillness resides both hesitation and hope: the yearning that vulnerability might bridge the distance between them. My works invite the viewer to linger on that threshold, to feel the fragile promise of connection as it pulses in the space between bodies, before it slips away.'
