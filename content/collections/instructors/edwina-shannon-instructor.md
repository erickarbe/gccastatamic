        text: "A graduate of Emmanuel College with a degree in studio art, Edwina continued calligraphy studies with E. Helene Sherman at Harvard University and Karen Gorst from the School of Sacred Arts in New York. Edwina has been a member of the Nashville Calligraphy Guild and International Association of Master Penman, Engrossers, and Teachers of Handwriting. She teaches calligraphy and basic design to both adults and children throughout the mid-TN area, including Watkins College of Art, Design & Film. She has scripted manuscripts, citations and promotional artwork for groups, associations and clubs as well as adopted lettering styles to create letter based logos for businesses.\_"
