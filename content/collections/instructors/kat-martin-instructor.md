        text: 'Kat Martin is an artist and educator based in South Carolina. She recently earned her Bachelor of Fine Arts in Art Education from the University of South Carolina. In 2023, she was awarded the Ethel S. Brody scholarship and was funded to attend the Penland School of Craft, where she extended her artistic practice to paper-making. She now primarily uses handmade paper and collage to explore themes of tension and connection between inner and outer worlds. Kat loves to host workshops that are opportunities for creative experimentation and expression. She has exhibited artwork across the Carolinas and has taught in museums, community centers, schools and at regional and state conferences.'
