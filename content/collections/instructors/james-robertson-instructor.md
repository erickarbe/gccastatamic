        text: 'James is a Scottish‐born artist based in Simpsonville, South Carolina, known for atmospheric, emotionally charged landscape paintings that explore the tension between presence and decay. Working primarily in oil, he builds quiet, immersive environments shaped by compressed values, tonal subtlety, and the recurring motif of dead and living trees. His paintings emphasize mood over spectacle, drawing viewers into spaces where light, weather, and memory converge. After relocating to the United States, James has been steadily rebuilding his artistic reputation while developing a cohesive body of work aimed at gallery representation. Alongside his studio practice, he teaches painting with a focus on value, perspective, and observational accuracy, helping students cultivate sensitivity and intention in their work. His current portfolio centers on large‐scale atmospheric landscapes that reflect both technical discipline and a deep personal connection to place.'
