        text: 'Artistically, I center my ancestral history, storytelling, and radical dreams to imagine an ideal world and question our current one. As an Art Historian, I join conversations surrounding colonialism and post-colonialism, drawing inspiration from many sources, especially Coco Fusco’s writing and Ana Mendieta’s performances. As Latina women, I empathize with their responses to violence, and hope to embody that resilience in my own work. I aim to create a body of work exploring loving, drawing from Judith Butler’s ‘Precarious Life’ and Surrealist thinking. My identity (queer, Latinx, female, and shifting) and relationship with my mother have shaped my beliefs about love. While all my work reflects love—of the environment, high school, or ancestral history—I now focus on moments where love has felt precarious, embodying the themes of precarious love and hope present in my life and work environment.'
