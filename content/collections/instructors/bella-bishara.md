        text: 'Bella Bishara is a Greenville-based multimedia fine artist and illustrator. She graduated from the South Carolina Governor’s School for the Arts and Humanities in Visual Arts. She also received education at the Savannah College of Art and Design. Though raised in Greenville, she is originally from Egypt. Her work combines different mediums including traditional acrylic painting, crochet and other fibers, beads, colored pencils, pastels, and other non traditional materials. She primarily uses a vibrant, bright color palette to separate the contents of her work from reality, keeping it living in an imaginary space. Bishara also uses embellishments and borders in her works partially inspired by Art Nouveau and historical religious artworks, though made into her own interpretations. Different themes of her work include identity, humanity, and mental illness. Though her subject matters and themes differ, her work shares a distinct, playful, intense style.'
