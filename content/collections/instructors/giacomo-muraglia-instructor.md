        text: "Giacomo Muraglia was born in Sicily on February 1989, since the age of 7, he has a great passion for American culture. In 2003 he enrolled at the Liceo Artistico Giuseppe Damiani Almeyda (Art School) in Palermo, where at the age of 15 he discovered another passion; the Sculpture. Once he finished the Art School, in 2008, he enrolled in the Sculpture Course of the Academy of Fine Arts in Palermo where, under the guidance of the Sicilian Master Sculptor Salvatore Rizzuti, he graduated in 2012 in Visual Arts and Disciplines of Entertainment. Over the years, Giacomo has worked in his Studio in Palermo, in various sculpture workshops and Comic Conventions all over Italy putting into practice his skills as a performer and entertainer for the audience creating life size sculptures for the event's guests of honor, but his real dream since he was a little kid is to move to America. Moved in U.S. in 2019, he currently works on commission as a sculptor painter and designer in Los Angeles, and as a sculptor instructor, living his Dream."
