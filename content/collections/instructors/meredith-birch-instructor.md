        text: 'Meredith Birch is a Greenville, South Carolina based mixed media artist and educator whose work explores emotional landscapes through layered abstraction. Inspired by themes of change, reflection, and lived experience, she creates intuitive compositions that use translucent color, expressive mark making, and form to investigate the inner terrain of personal transformation. With more than a decade of experience as an art educator in Massachusetts and South Carolina, Birch brings both a process driven approach to her studio practice and a strong technical foundation to her teaching. She believes creative exploration invites curiosity, encourages personal expression, and fosters meaningful connection. Birch is expanding her studio practice through new bodies of work that explore resilience, transition, and the stories carried within layered surfaces. Through her artwork and teaching, she creates opportunities for others to engage with art as a means of reflection, connection, and discovery.'
