**About:**

Canvas is a small business located in Greenville, SC. A husband and wife team, Arin & Genavieve Gilbert are dedicated to empowering makers of all kinds. Arin is a coffee roaster, lover of music & people, with a background in community and event organization. Genavieve is a fine artist, author, and teacher with a heart to empower other creatives. Together, our mission at Canvas is to empower makers of all kinds by providing platforms for others to showcase and share their creative work with the world.
