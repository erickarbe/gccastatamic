            text: "For Pete, the keyword is enjoyment. His artistic journey is fueled by a lifelong fascination with the world's fragments, rooted in an early affinity for Philadelphia’s gritty urban landscapes, where he began his artistic journey. With 15 years of experience as a Master Docent at the Telfair Museums in Savannah, Pete blends classical painting and drawing traditions with a modern photographic vision. He eschews rigid formal theory, believing that true creativity is a dialogue between the eye and the heart. This artistic intuition is backed by his background as an electronics engineer, a technical foundation that ensures his gear remains a tool for his vision, never a barrier. From the quiet structure of a still life to the kinetic energy of a city street, Pete seeks the natural essence in every frame."
