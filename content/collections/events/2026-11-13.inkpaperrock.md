            text: 'Pang’s approach is shaped by his personal experience of navigating two cultures. Born in Greenville, SC to a Chinese father and an American mother, he has spent much of his life exploring the relationship between these backgrounds. He received his bachelor’s degree in Painting and Drawing from the University of Washington in Seattle before moving to China, where he spent three years studying and traveling throughout the country and other parts of Asia. After returning to the United States, he earned his Master of Fine Arts degree from the University of Chicago. He has just recently moved back to Upstate, SC. His work draws from traditional Chinese brush painting and tools, as well as ideas from Western philosophy and geometry.'
