In 2024, she created Brooklynn Ceramics, where she specialized in teaching women across South Carolina the art of ceramics and hand-building. She is rooted in community, self-discovery, and creativity all through the art of clay.\_"

        text: "Finding a genuine connection from a person to her art is the ultimate reward for Brooke. Those moments and conversations continue to inspire and give reason to the “why” of her creations. With GCCA’s new ceramics studio opening and upcoming classes on the horizon, Brooke opens a space “where people [can] reconnect with creativity, curiosity, and themselves.” Many are bound by the belief that they aren't creative – only to see that belief disappear when they begin to play and work with clay, for Brooke it is truly the best part of teaching. Ceramics is welcoming to all, whether you have years of experience or none; clay shows us to slow down, play, and to be inspired by all things.\_"
