        text: "In the bustling art world of Greenville, South Carolina, I am honored to both interview and introduce Dylan Swain, a visual artist who specializes in 2D and 3D design. In the past years at the Greenville Center for Creative Arts, Dylan has exhibited his works in the 2021 ' Seven Places ' exhibition as well as some First Fridays events. Artwork cultivated by Swain was motif-inspired by the nature around him, both big and small, from invasive insect species to the Great Lakes of Texas. In this interview, the reader shall get to know Dylan Swain not only through his works but as the person he is as a whole."
